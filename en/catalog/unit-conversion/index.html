Scientific Unit Conversion

Convert between units of the same quantity, such as Celsius and Kelvin.

Science / Utility

What it does

  • Convert length, mass, time, volume, energy and pressure
  • Handle temperature scales with an offset
  • Flag unknown units and incompatible dimensions

When to use it

  • Express lab records in the units used in a paper
  • Compare values reported in different units

Current status

The supported quantities and conversion rules are documented.
